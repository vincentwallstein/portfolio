On the Aerodynamics and Chassis subteams, I test carbon fiber alternatives for push rods, tie rods, and toe links.

I designed and 3D printed the test jigs and inserts. The parts come in several lengths, diameters, and thicknesses, so the main challenge was designing a universal jig that reduces how many unique jigs we need. I also helped develop the testing procedure and timeline for validation.

Starlight Cardiovascular / May to July 2026

Nitinol Stent Analysis

I ran regression and statistical modeling on 65+ nitinol stents to measure how manufacturing parameters affect mechanical performance, and presented the findings to company leadership.

I then designed a 2-factor full factorial DOE to set FDA-defensible manufacturing tolerances for pediatric cardiac stents. A manufacturing constraint created an orthogonality conflict, so I restructured the design to work around it.

Role
Intern, Nitinol Engineer
Tools
Minitab, Excel, DOE, regression
Result
Findings presented to company leadership
